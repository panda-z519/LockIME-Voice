## What's changed

### Fixed: upgrading 0.1.4 could leave the voice shortcut doing nothing

0.1.4 shipped with a bug that made the shortcut feel dead, and there were two
separate causes:

- **The voice key stayed disabled after you opened Settings.** The tap that takes
  over your voice key was told to stand down whenever the Voice Input pane was on
  screen, and that state could stick — so afterwards the key fell through to your
  input method (the conflict prompt) or did nothing at all. The tap now decides
  per key press whether Settings is actually focused, and re-arms itself as soon
  as you close the window. Verified in all three states: Settings closed →
  consumes the key; Settings open → yields it to the recorder; closed again →
  consumes it again.
- **The Accessibility grant is lost on every update.** This preview is ad-hoc
  signed, so a new build is a new signature and macOS no longer recognises the
  permission you granted to the previous one. **After installing this version you
  must grant Accessibility again** (see below). 0.1.4 also failed to make this
  obvious, which is why the shortcut appeared to do nothing.

### Fixed: recording a shortcut could still fight your input method

While the recorder has focus, LockIME now yields the key instead of swallowing
it, so the press reaches the recorder. Recording no longer leaves the tap
permanently disabled, which is what made the key unusable afterwards.

## Install

Ad-hoc signed and **not notarized**. Two permission steps, both required:

1. **First launch** — Gatekeeper blocks it. Open **System Settings ▸ Privacy &
   Security**, scroll to **Security**, and click **Open Anyway** for LockIME
   Voice.
2. **Grant Accessibility** — in **System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸
   Accessibility**, enable LockIME Voice. This is required for LockIME to take
   over your voice shortcut. If an older entry is still listed, remove it first
   and add the new build, because the ad-hoc signature changed.

Then record your voice shortcut once in **Settings ▸ Voice Input** and press it
to dictate.

If the shortcut still does nothing, open **Settings ▸ Voice Input**: the pane
reports when the key could not be taken over, which almost always means the
Accessibility grant is missing or stale.
